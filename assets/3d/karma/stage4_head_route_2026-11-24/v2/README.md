# Head v2 (EXPERIMENT, not final, likeness NOT verified)
Dense geometric head (39k verts): almond eye openings cut into the skin with upper and lower lid rims, 3D eyeballs behind them (iris partly under the upper lid), mouth opening cut into the skin with lip tubes, teeth row and cavity, geometry nose, round cheeks, small chin. Brows and blush are vertex colour. No planar photo/paint texture.
Convergence check (metric_v2_summary.txt): MediaPipe run on the rendered front view and on the front reference, both levelled and scaled by pupil distance. v1 to v2 mean landmark error went 0.049 to 0.041 pupil-distances; face width, eye width, nose width, chin depth, cheek and eye spacing match within about 5 percent. CAVEAT: MediaPipe fits a template to stylized renders, so this checks layout consistency only. It does not measure likeness.
Known defects: side profile is not trustworthy (side and back depth are guesses): lips/nose protrude oddly, no ears, no hair yet, expression slightly stiff, shading flat, not attached to the body or skinned. Reference-photo comparison images are kept local and are not in the repo.
Run: python3 build2.py && python3 r2.py (needs numpy, scipy; metric.py needs mediapipe, opencv and the local reference).
