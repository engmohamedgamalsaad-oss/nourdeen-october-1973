# Head v2 (EXPERIMENT, not final, likeness NOT verified)
Dense geometric head (88k verts): almond eye openings with upper/lower lid rims over a skin-coloured inner layer, 3D eyeballs behind them (iris partly under the upper lid), nose, round cheeks, small chin. Mouth: lips are surface displacement; the opening is a smooth patch built from the exact smile curves with one continuous upper tooth strip (tooth gaps are colour lines) and dark interior, edged by thin rim tubes that follow the surface. Brows and blush are vertex colour. No photo or paint texture.
Correction: an earlier version of this README said the layout matched within about 5 percent. That was wrong. Summary numbers are in metric_v2_summary.txt (MediaPipe on the render vs the front reference, levelled, in pupil-distance units; mean error about 0.04). Eye width, eye spacing, face width, chin depth and nose width are close; mouth width is 1.00 vs 0.95. This only checks layout consistency, MediaPipe fits a template to cartoon renders, and it says nothing about likeness.
GUESSED (not visible in the references): all side and back depth, skull shape behind the face, nose projection, chin and jaw profile. Profile and 3/4 views are therefore unverified.
Known defects: no hair, no ears, nose has too little volume, lips too wide and pale, brows too thick and high, eyes slightly round, shading flat. Not attached to the body, not skinned. Reference-photo comparison images are kept local and are not in the repo.
Run: python3 build2.py && python3 r2.py
