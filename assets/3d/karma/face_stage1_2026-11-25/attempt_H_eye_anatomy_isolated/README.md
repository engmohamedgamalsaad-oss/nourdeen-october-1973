# attempt H: isolated eye proof only (one right-eye patch, NOT a whole head). EXPERIMENT_NOT_FINAL. G and earlier untouched.
Representation: a real eyeball ellipsoid (.35x.17x.21 IPD, yawed 14 degrees to follow the skin slope) with a real convex cornea dome, sitting in the face. The lid is the offset shell of that eyeball (thickness .028) blended into the face skin; the eye aperture (reference outline) is cut through that shell only. The lid margin is therefore the cut edge of the shell, rounded .012, with the real eyeball visible behind it. Same SDF framework, different construction: the eyeball is solid geometry, the lid wraps it.
Reproduce: cd src; EYEMODE=anat ANG=14 python3 eye_patch.py
Known faults: iris and pupil are flat colour on the cornea with a pink fringe at its edge; gaze points 14 degrees outward; the inner corner shows a dark wedge (lid thickness shadow); lid margin is slightly jagged at grid .008; no lashes/gloss; upper lid does not overlap the iris. Not yet built on the whole head.
